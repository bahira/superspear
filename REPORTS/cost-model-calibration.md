# Cost-model calibration (measured) — NEGATIVE RESULT, NOT ADOPTED

> **These weights were rejected.** Tested end-to-end against the 22
> wall-clock-measured kernels, they predict real hardware *worse* than the
> a priori weights: median `|log(modelled/measured)|` rose from **0.097 to
> 0.177**. `OP_COST` is unchanged.
>
> The harness times ops in a serial dependency chain, so it measures
> **latency**. Real kernels pipeline independent subexpressions, so their
> runtime is governed by **throughput**. `sqrt` and `pdiv` look ~5x dearer
> under latency than they behave in practice. Fitting `OP_COST` needs
> whole-kernel measurements (`bench-wallclock.ts`), not isolated ops.
>
> Kept as a documented dead end so the experiment is not silently redone.
